Spring planting guide

Spring is the busiest season in the garden. This guide covers soil preparation, choosing seedlings and watering schedules for raised beds.

Preparing the soil

Turn the soil to a depth of 20 cm and mix in compost from our shared bins. Read our composting basics.

What to plant

  • Tomatoes
  • Beans
  • Lettuce
  • Herbs
